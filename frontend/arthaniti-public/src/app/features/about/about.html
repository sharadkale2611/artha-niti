About Malegaon Nagar Panchayat

Serving Citizens with Transparency and Responsibility

Malegaon Nagar Panchayat is committed to providing essential civic services and improving the quality of life of citizens. The Nagar Panchayat works towards the development of the city through effective administration and citizen participation.

Our objective is to make municipal services easily accessible, transparent and convenient for every citizen through both traditional and digital service delivery.

Our Commitment

Better services, transparent administration and sustainable development for our citizens.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Working together for a better and developed Malegaon.

Our Vision

To develop Malegaon as a clean, healthy, sustainable and citizen-friendly urban area with modern civic infrastructure and efficient public services.

Our Mission

To provide reliable municipal services, promote transparency, encourage citizen participation and ensure effective implementation of development initiatives.
